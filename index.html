Privacy Policy — Card Centering Inspector

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Card Centering Inspector is a browser extension that measures trading-card centering in photos, estimates a grade, and compares listing prices with recent sales. Everything it saves stays on your computer. It sends no data to the developer or to anyone else.

What the extension saves, and where

Only in your browser's local extension storage, on your own computer:

  • Comps you save or collect: card names, sale prices, shipping, sale dates, and the titles of the sold listings shown on eBay pages you opened.
  • eBay Price Guide figures, read from the listing page when you open its "See insights" panel.
  • Your settings: grading company, fees and costs, minimum profit.

Nothing is synced to Google or any other server. Uninstalling the extension deletes this data. The extension can export your data to a file on your computer only when you click its Export button.

What the extension reads

It runs only on eBay pages and on local files you open (such as a report made by the companion script). On those pages it reads the listing's title, price, shipping, condition and location, and the listing photos, to show you the centering, estimated grade and price comparison. Photos are analyzed inside the extension on your computer.

Network use

  • To analyze a listing photo, the extension downloads that photo from eBay's image server, the same photo your browser is already showing. It's analyzed locally and not sent anywhere.
  • When you click "Get sold comps", it opens an eBay sold-search page in a new visible tab, one search per click. It never loads pages in hidden tabs or loops through searches by itself.
  • It never buys, bids, makes offers, watches items, sends messages or submits forms on eBay.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, cookies or third-party services.
  • No collection of personal, financial or account information.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data to share: none leaves your computer.

Estimates

Grades, prices and verdicts are estimates from photos and recent sales. They are not official grades and not financial advice.

Changes

If what the extension saves or reads ever changes, this page and the extension's changelog will say so before the update is released.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
